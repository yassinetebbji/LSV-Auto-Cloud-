Privacy Policy

LSV Auto Cloud ("we" or "us") provides automotive website and CRM services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form data: name, email, phone number, business, team size, and the message you provide.
  • Demo bookings: when you schedule through Cal.com, we receive booking details (name, email, selected time, notes). Cal.com may collect additional data under their own privacy terms.
  • Usage and device data: through Google Analytics (GA4) we receive information such as pages viewed, referrers, approximate location, and device/browser details. GA4 uses cookies and similar technologies.
  • Communications: emails or calls you send to us.

How we use information

  • Provide and improve our website and services.
  • Respond to inquiries, schedule demos, and deliver requested information.
  • Send service-related updates or marketing (you can opt out of marketing at any time).
  • Analyze site performance and improve user experience.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, or security risks, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing of information

  • Service providers: vendors who help us operate the site and deliver services (e.g., hosting, email, analytics, scheduling via Cal.com). They access data only to perform services for us.
  • Legal and safety: if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may transfer as part of the transaction.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and site functionality. You can manage cookies in your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some site features.

Data retention

We retain information as long as needed for the purposes above, to meet legal obligations, or to resolve disputes. We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer necessary.

Your choices

  • Marketing opt-out: you can opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.
  • Analytics controls: manage cookies in your browser or use tools like Google’s opt-out add-on for GA.
  • Access, update, delete: you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

International transfers

We operate from Canada and may process data in other countries where our service providers are located. We take steps to protect data in accordance with this Policy.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date will reflect the most recent changes.

Contact

If you have questions or requests, contact us at contact@lsvautocloud.com.
